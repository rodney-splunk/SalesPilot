# Data boundaries and pilot review

Raw audio is processed locally. Cisco mode can transmit selected transcript text and public evidence to the existing Cisco provider. Contact masking reduces known identifiers but is not complete DLP. Local-only mode blocks hosted AI, provider authentication and public documentation retrieval. Public competitor reads use fixed official URLs, not customer questions. Internal enablement links are for human review.

Consent is required before capture. The consent checkbox records the operator’s attestation and does not establish the applicable legal requirements. Review notices and consent rules with your organization. No automatic customer email, CRM write or Slack write is triggered by the workspace.

Local session archives, prep, reference indexes and journals contain confidential information; keep them out of Git and unapproved sync/backups. No approved universal retention period or automatic purge is supplied. Credentials are provisioned individually through macOS Keychain. Do not share Rodney’s credentials, signing keys, call history or local configuration.

Formal decisions remain open: permitted data classification/CIRCUIT terms, consent and retention, backup destinations, endpoint baseline, dependency/model provenance and managed distribution. Existing synthetic checks are engineering evidence, not compliance approval.

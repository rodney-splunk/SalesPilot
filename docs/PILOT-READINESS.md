## Pilot decisions and exit criteria

1. Assign a sponsor, maintainer/backup and support/incident route; confirm overlap with existing enablement and engineering tools.
2. Obtain the applicable internal-tool review path, approved data categories, CIRCUIT model/region/logging/retention/training terms, budget owner and limits. Approved provider access alone does not approve this use case.
3. Confirm managed storage/backup, retention/holds, endpoint permissions, code-host/distribution and exact model/license provenance. No silent migration, deletion or new egress.
4. Start with a small authorized group and a fixed workload. Compare with unassisted discovery and expert review. Measure useful follow-ups, technical/business/personal depth, note completeness, speaker corrections, readable citation support and user-visible latency/failure rates. Assess whether users learn and retain discovery judgment.
5. Establish stop/rollback rules, release review triggers and second-Mac onboarding acceptance. Broader deployment requires its own decision.

## Bounded evaluation proposal for the ownership discussion

Proposed scope, subject to the responsible owner’s approval: 3–5 volunteer SEs, fictional practice first, then only explicitly authorized call data; two weeks with a recorded release and fixed scenarios. No administrative personnel scoring. Compare assisted and unassisted discovery using blinded review where practical.

| Measure | Ground truth and denominator | Proposed acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Follow-up quality | Every displayed cue reviewed against preceding conversation; separate technical, business and personal depth | At least 80% judged useful and natural; no unsupported pivot presented as fact |
| Question completion | All customer questions annotated manually, including interruptions and refinements | No silently dropped question in the agreed acceptance fixtures; open/answered/deferred state matches evidence |
| Notes | Each confirmed fact compared with transcript and correction history | No invented confirmed fact; at least 90% of annotated material facts retained |
| Methodology | Evidence for each partial/completed point | No completion without customer evidence; show remaining gaps |
| Sourced answers | Each material claim checked against the displayed source/version | No unsupported material claim; absence of approved CI explicitly visible |
| Response time | Speech end to visible output, including hold and queue; cold/warm separately | Report p50/p95 and failures; targets remain provisional until measured on presentation hardware |
| Reliability | All attempted runs, including failures and cancellations | No cross-call contamination, capture after stop or hidden failure |

These are proposed evaluation thresholds, not measured results or organizational policy. Pause the pilot for unintended data transfer, unexplained attribution, lost accepted turns, persistent capture after stop or materially unsupported answers. Preserve evidence, assign a maintainer and use the documented rollback.

The ownership conversation should resolve overlap with existing efforts, sponsor/technical owner, review route, approved data and provider terms, support responsibility and whether to authorize this bounded evaluation.
